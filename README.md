# App-Development-and-MVP-Launching-Project-Management
Web App aggregator platform for Essential Health care Product and Services Market place Wep App in Nigeria
This is a link to the Medlinkr App, a tech MVP, that was developed and launched by a Project team which i led as Project Manager and Business Analyst.
The Medlinkr App is a health tech servicing platform for Medbury Medical Services, which is a niche healthcare institution with a mission to build a healthcare ecosystem by aggregating patient-centered providers of medical, pharmaceutical and ancillary services in within the healthcare value chain in order to provide wholistic care to clients home abroad.

To make access to common healthcare services easier and more efficient, we have developed the MEdlinkr App, an all-in-one Web App that hosts medical, pharmaceutical, beauty, wellness, emergency response and telemedicine services in dedicated ecommerce online stores where customers can purchase their desired afore mentioned health care products and services on the go. It can be downloaded for free from the Appstore and Play store.
The platform was desgined to provide access to quality healthcare products and services to our esteemed users of the platform
There are 3 main modules on the App which are the, Health Shop comprising of the Pharmacy, Beauty and Body Care products and Wellness stores, Laboratory & Diagnostics, as well as, Medical Services including Emergency Response, Telemedicine and Fitness services
